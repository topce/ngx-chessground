Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

ngx-chessground does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

  • All chess analysis (Stockfish engine) runs entirely locally on your device.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party services, APIs, or SDKs are used.
  • No internet connection is required (except for the WebView2 runtime on the Windows desktop build).

Local Storage

  • App preferences (e.g., board theme, piece style, language) may be stored locally on your device using browser storage or local file storage.
  • This data never leaves your device.
  • You can clear this data at any time through your browser or app settings.

Third-Party Services

This application does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If you use the "Open on Lichess" feature, you will be directed to the Lichess website, which has its own privacy policy. You are not required to use this feature.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
