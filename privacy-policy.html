Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

ngx-chessground does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

  • All chess analysis (Stockfish engine) runs entirely locally on your device
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind
  • No third-party services, APIs, or SDKs are used
  • No internet connection is required (except for the WebView2 runtime on Windows)

Local Storage

  • App preferences (e.g. board theme, piece style) may be stored locally in your browser's storage
  • This data never leaves your device

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, open an issue on GitHub.
